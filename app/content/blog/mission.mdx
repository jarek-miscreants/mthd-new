As AI accelerates, cyber offense is scaling to infinity. Nation-states and criminal groups are already weaponizing these advances into machine-speed attacks that threaten our way of life.

America’s essential institutions now have no choice but to achieve true cyber resilience. Their vast attack surfaces are no longer protected by obscurity. To survive in a world of continuous, autonomous attacks, they must safely and scalably wield these new threats against themselves - before adversaries do.

The Cyber Industrial Complex was not built for this moment. The industry has become optimized for acquisitions and acronyms, not delivering resilience at scale. More fragmented point solutions, service providers, and endless integrations will not move the needle. Security teams need entirely new systems that blend offense, defense, software, and autonomy to ensure readiness.

**Method builds autonomous cyber systems for America’s top security teams.**

Our products fuse offensive and defensive capabilities into a full-spectrum platform, enabling security teams to achieve complete visibility, challenge their estates, and defeat their adversaries.

Method gathers and fuses diverse data sources into a digital twin, giving operators an interconnected map of resources, configurations, and exposures. This digital twin serves as the basis for operators to conduct autonomous attacks with extreme precision and trust.

Purpose-built for complex security environments, Method performs across cloud, on-premise, and internet-denied networks. It’s autonomy is designed around safety, trust, and performance - with the rules of engagement and human-in-the-loop required for no-fail environments.

We built Method as dual-use from day one. Cyber conflict does not discriminate between the public and private sector, so neither can our defenses. We are proud to support customers in the Department of Defense, US Federal Government, and Fortune 500.

We’ve raised $26M in a Seed and Series A from Andreessen Horowitz, General Catalyst, Blackstone, and leaders in security and AI.

Our founding team comes from Palantir, Shield AI, NSA, USAF, CrowdStrike, Microsoft, and AWS.

We are building for the people who defend what matters most, and hiring outstanding people who share that mission.
