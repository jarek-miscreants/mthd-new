Personal Information: We may collect personal information that you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and company details, when you create an account, contact us, or request information about our Services.
Automatically Collected Information: We collect certain information automatically when you use our website, such as IP addresses, browser type, device identifiers, and browsing activity.
Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information and improve user experience.

We use the information we collect to:

- To protect the security and integrity of the Sites and our business, such as preventing fraud, hacking, and other criminal activity or to meet legal obligations.

We may share your information with:
